Happy Girlfriend's Day Manaar

A name that means guiding light — and somehow, still undersells it.

a letter, unfolding

My Manaar,

There is a particular kind of blue that only exists right before the sky decides whether it's going to be night or morning — not quite dark, not quite light, just quiet and enormous and full of possibility. That is the color I think of when I think of you.

I did not know, before you, that a person could be a direction. That someone could simply exist, somewhere in the world, and I would find myself oriented toward them the way a ship finds true north — not because I was lost, but because knowing where you are makes everywhere else make sense.

Your name means guiding light, and I used to think that was just a lovely coincidence, a nice fact to mention on days like today. I don't think that anymore. I think some people are named honestly, and you are one of them. You have a way of walking into a room, or a conversation, or an ordinary Tuesday, and quietly making it easier to see.

I love the parts of you that are loud — your laugh that turns heads, your opinions that don't apologize for existing, the way you fight for the people you love like it's the easiest decision in the world. And I love the parts of you that are quiet — the way you go still when you're really listening, the small kindnesses you think no one notices, the softness you save for the people lucky enough to be let in.

Today is just a day on a calendar, invented by no one in particular, for exactly this reason — so that once a year, I have an excuse this specific to stop and say it plainly: I am so lucky to be loved by you. I don't take it for granted, not for one ordinary Tuesday of it.

So happy Girlfriend's Day, Manaar. Here's to the blue hour, and to you — the one I keep finding my way back to.
